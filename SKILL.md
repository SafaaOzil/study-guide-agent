# Study Guide Skill

## Purpose

This skill defines how the Study Guide Agent should prepare
study material for the student.

## Instructions

When creating a study guide:

1. Use only information that appears in the uploaded course material.
2. Do not invent information that is not in the source.
3. Write short and clear explanations.
4. Divide the material into clear topics.
5. Identify important concepts and definitions.
6. Highlight important points that should be remembered for an exam.
7. When possible, include simple examples based on the source material.
8. At the end of each topic, create 2-3 review questions.
9. Finish the document with a section called:
   "Things I Must Remember".
10. Keep the study guide organized and easy to read.
